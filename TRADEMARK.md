# Business Entity Trademark and Brand Policy

This document defines the public brand boundary for Business Entity Engine.

This policy is not a software license. The software license is the MIT License in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Protected Project Identity

The following names and identifiers represent the official Business Entity project identity:

- Business Entity
- Business Entity Engine
- Business Entity Platform
- Business Entity Cloud
- Business Entity Enterprise
- Business Entity Plugins
- BusinessEntity
- official Business Entity logos, icons, visual style and brand assets
- official Business Entity domains, repositories, release channels and distribution channels

These names and assets are not licensed under the MIT License.

## Source Code License Boundary

The MIT License allows broad use of the source code and documentation, including copying, modification, distribution, sublicensing and sale, subject to the license terms.

The MIT License does not grant permission to use the Business Entity name, logo, icon, visual identity or official status in a way that implies endorsement, sponsorship, certification, partnership or official affiliation.

## Allowed Uses

You may use the Business Entity name truthfully to describe compatibility, origin or relationship to the project.

Allowed examples:

- "Fork of Business Entity Engine"
- "Based on Business Entity Engine"
- "Built on Business Entity Engine"
- "Powered by Business Entity Engine"
- "Compatible with Business Entity"
- "Unofficial plugin for Business Entity"
- "Unofficial community build of Business Entity Engine"
- "We provide consulting for Business Entity deployments"

These uses must be accurate and must not imply that your project, service or package is official unless you have written permission.

## Not Allowed Without Written Permission

Do not use the Business Entity name, logo, icon or visual identity in a way that can confuse users about who operates, publishes, approves or supports a product or service.

Not allowed examples:

- "Official Business Entity"
- "Business Entity Cloud"
- "Business Entity Pro"
- "Business Entity Enterprise"
- "Business Entity Hosting"
- "Business Entity Certified"
- "Business Entity Foundation"
- using official logos or icons for a fork or derived product
- using domains or social accounts that imply official status
- publishing packages or container images that look like official releases
- presenting a modified build as an official Business Entity release

## Forks and Derived Projects

Forks and derived projects are welcome, but they should use a distinct name and identity.

If your project is based on Business Entity Engine, make that relationship clear and add a visible disclaimer such as:

```text
This is an unofficial fork of Business Entity Engine.
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by the official Business Entity project.
```

## Plugins and Integrations

Plugins, integrations and compatible modules may refer to Business Entity to describe their purpose.

Recommended naming patterns:

- "Unofficial plugin for Business Entity"
- "Business Entity-compatible module"
- "Integration for Business Entity Engine"

Avoid names that imply official ownership, certification or exclusive status.

## Official Releases

Only repositories, packages, container images, websites and release artifacts explicitly published by the Business Entity maintainers are official Business Entity releases.

Community builds, forks and private deployments must identify themselves as unofficial when distributed outside their organization.

## Brand Assets

Do not copy, modify or redistribute official Business Entity logos, icons or visual identity assets for a fork, service, product, package or organization without written permission.

You may use the Business Entity name in plain text to refer to the official project, compatibility or dependency relationship, as described in this policy.

## Contact

For brand permissions, certification language, partnership language or official distribution requests, contact the project maintainers.
